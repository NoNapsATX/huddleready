What it does when the lights go out

One big button, then one step at a time. Made for a dark room, one free hand, and a toddler on your hip.

Walks you through the first 10 minutes

Check on everyone, find the flashlights, report the outage, and protect the fridge, in that order.

Tells you whether to stay or go

Answer a few taps about the temperature inside and what still works, and it says whether home is still safe for your kids.

Keeps time on the fridge and freezer

A countdown from the moment the power went out, so you know what is still safe to eat.

Kids Mode with a real voice

A short story and games about the dark for little kids. A flashlight hunt, a safe-or-not quiz, and learning your phone number.

Set it up once, before you need it

About five minutes on a normal day, with the internet on.

  1. Answer five questions

    Your ZIP code, your kind of home, who lives there, any medical needs, and pets.

  2. Get your family's plan

    Checklists, supplies and nearby places to go, written for your home and your kids' ages.

  3. Add it to your home screen

    iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app. From then on it opens with no signal and no Wi-Fi.

Open the app once with internet to set it up. No account, no app store, nothing to pay.

Our promises

Always free

No ads, no subscription, no paid tier. Donations only ever cover the web address and a safety review.

Your details stay on your phone

No account, no tracking. What you type into the app is saved on your phone and nowhere else.

Built for Central Texas

Ice storms, summer heat, flash floods, and the local numbers to call when they knock the power out.

Safety advice from the people who write it

Guidance comes from the American Red Cross, the CDC and Ready.gov, and each screen names its source.
