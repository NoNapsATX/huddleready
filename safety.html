Safety

In an emergency, call 911. If you can't call, you can text 911 in Austin and the surrounding Central Texas counties. Call if you can.

What Huddle Ready is

Huddle Ready is a planning aid made by a family, for families. It helps you get ready before an outage and keeps your plan in reach when the internet is down. It is not medical advice, and it doesn't replace a doctor, 911 or local officials.

Official orders come first

If city, county or state officials tell you to evacuate, boil water or stay off the roads, do what they say, even if the app suggests something else. To get local alerts, sign up at WarnCentralTexas.org.

Where the advice comes from

Safety steps in the app use guidance from these groups, and each screen names its source:

  • American Red Cross: power outages, first aid basics
  • CDC: food safety, carbon monoxide, extreme heat and cold
  • Ready.gov (FEMA): planning and supply kits
  • Ready Central Texas: local hazards and resources

Medicines

The app never lists medicine doses. It reminds you which medicines to keep on hand, then tells you to follow the product label or ask your child's doctor or pharmacist.

How we check it

Before launch we are asking local emergency and preparedness educators to review the safety screens. If they can't, we will pay a licensed clinician to review them. We will list who reviewed it, and when, on this page.

Found a mistake?

Please email hello@huddleready.com. Safety corrections go out before anything else.
