Privacy

The short version

Huddle Ready has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. What you type into the app stays on your phone. We never see it.

What the app saves, and where

Things like your phone numbers, building and unit, checklist ticks and the outage timer are saved in your phone's browser storage, inside the app. They are not sent anywhere.

  • Clearing your browser data, or deleting the app from your home screen, erases them.
  • Two phones in the same family each keep their own copy.
  • Nobody else can see them, including us.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, login or email needed to use the app.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and no cookies.
  • No location tracking. The app only asks for your location if you tap "Find help near me" (see below).
  • We never sell or share information, because we don't collect any.

Find help near me

This is optional. If you tap it, your phone shares its location with the app once. The app rounds it to about half a mile and sends only that rounded spot to OpenStreetMap's free public lookup service (Overpass) to find nearby emergency rooms, fire stations, pharmacies and libraries. The results are saved on your phone. Your location is not saved, and nothing is sent to us. Place data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Tapping a directions button opens Google Maps with that place as the destination. That's covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Sharing your plan

If you share your plan with family, your details travel inside the link itself. Nothing is stored on a server. Anyone who has the link can see what's in it, so send it only to people you trust.

The website host

This website and the app are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security. We don't receive those logs. See GitHub's note on data collection.

If you email us

If you write to hello@huddleready.com, we use your email only to reply, and to schedule your test if you asked to join one. We don't add you to a mailing list.

Kids

Kids Mode collects nothing. The voice is recorded audio stored inside the app, so nothing your child does leaves the phone.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page first and say what changed at the top.
