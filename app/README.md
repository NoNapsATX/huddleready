# Huddle Ready app

Power's out. Huddle up.

The free Huddle Ready app: five setup questions, then a "Power is out" walkthrough, stay-or-go helper, food clock, call list, the full plan, a supply checklist, and a Kids Mode with a recorded voice.

Open https://nonapsatx.github.io/huddleready/app/ once with internet and add it to the home screen. After that it works with no internet. Details typed into the app stay on that phone.
